# fusion-console

**Kind**: app (host/consumer) · **Source repo**: `dahai80/fusion-console`

Local console: manage fusion services, models and logs in one place.

本地控制台：一键管理 fusion 服务、模型与日志。

## Install

Download the archive for your platform from the
[releases page](https://github.com/dahai80/fusion-console/releases) and install locally.
Host apps are consumers of the plugin/connector/skill catalog, not entries of
it — they read `registry.json` and install entries on their own.
