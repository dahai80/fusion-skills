# fusion-cowork

**Kind**: app (host/consumer) · **Source repo**: `dahai80/fusion-cowork`

DAG workflow engine with a collaboration UI: orchestrate multi-step tasks visually.

DAG 工作流引擎与协作界面：把多步任务编排成可视化流程。

## Install

Download the archive for your platform from the
[releases page](https://github.com/dahai80/fusion-cowork/releases) and install locally.
Host apps are consumers of the plugin/connector/skill catalog, not entries of
it — they read `registry.json` and install entries on their own.
